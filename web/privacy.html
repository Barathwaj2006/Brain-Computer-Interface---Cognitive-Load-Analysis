1. Information Collected & Processed

During operation of the NeuroSim clinical workstation, the following categories of data may be handled:

  • Clinician Profile Metadata: Full name, institutional email address, and role for One-Time Password (OTP) verification and cryptographic session token generation.
  • Electrophysiological Telemetry: Microvolt biopotential values (Leads E1, E2, E3, and Aux sensor), packet sequence counters, timestamps, and hardware checksums.
  • Spectral & Cognitive Metrics: Radix-2 FFT power spectral densities across standard bands (Delta 0.5–4Hz, Theta 4–8Hz, Alpha 8–13Hz, Beta 13–30Hz), Theta/Beta Ratios (TBR), Alpha/Beta Ratios (ABR), and Neuro-Autonomic Stress Indices.
  • Cryptographic Audit Records: Client IP address, session start/stop timestamps, electrode impedance logs, and SHA-256 hash chains.

2. Storage Architecture & Data Sovereignty

Telemetry sessions and clinical records reside within your dedicated database environment — either locally via SQLite (WAL mode) or in your designated Supabase PostgreSQL instance with Row Level Security (RLS). NeuroSim enforces complete data sovereignty: no centralized vendor server retains copies of your raw neural recordings.

3. Security & Cryptographic Safeguards

NeuroSim enforces comprehensive physical, technical, and algorithmic security controls:

  • Hardware Packet Validation: Real-time checksum verification on all incoming 250 Hz UDP datagrams to eliminate corrupted or injected packets.
  • Rate Limiting & Quota Protection: Sliding-window token bucket rate limiter preventing brute-force authentication and denial-of-service disruptions.
  • Secure Session Tokens: Authenticated sessions utilize CSPRNG cryptographically secure pseudo-random number generator tokens (48 hex characters).
  • Transport Security: All API communication requires TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption and secure WebSocket (WSS) streaming in production.

4. Data Portability & Right to Erasure

Researchers and clinical operators retain 100% ownership over all patient records. You may export telemetry at any time via RFC 4180 CSV, structured JSON, binary EDF+, or HL7 FHIR DiagnosticReport bundles. Sessions can be permanently deleted from the workstation archive at any time with immediate cascading cleanup.

5. Contact & Governance Inquiries

For inquiries regarding biometric compliance, institutional review board (IRB) documentation, or clinical integration, refer to the official repository or contact your institution's data protection officer.
