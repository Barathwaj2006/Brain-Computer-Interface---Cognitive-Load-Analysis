CLINICAL PRIVACY & DATA GOVERNANCE

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 18, 2026 | Revision: 2.4-PRODUCTION
Notice Regarding Research & Biometric Telemetry: NeuroSim operates as a local-first clinical electroencephalography (EEG) analytics platform. All biometric and voltage time-series data captured from hardware devices (via laptop Wi-Fi UDP port 5005) is processed locally inside your runtime environment and is not transmitted to external third-party servers.

1. Information Collected

During standard operation of the NeuroSim platform, the following data categories may be collected and stored locally:

  • Authentication Metadata: Full name, institutional email address, and mobile phone number for secure One-Time Password (OTP) verification and cryptographic session token generation.
  • Electrophysiological Telemetry: Microvolt values, sequence indices, timestamp epochs, and mathematical checksums streamed from connected hardware sensors.
  • Clinical Analytics: Spectral power densities (Delta, Theta, Alpha, Beta), Theta/Beta ratios, Alpha/Beta ratios, and calculated cognitive engagement metrics.
  • System Audit Logs: Client IP address, session timestamps, hardware connection events, and diagnostic error reports.

2. Storage and Data Retention

All recorded telemetry sessions and user credentials reside exclusively within the local encrypted SQLite database (db/neurosim.db) on your host machine. We do not maintain off-site cloud mirrors of your raw EEG recordings unless explicitly configured by your institution.

3. Security & Cryptographic Safeguards

NeuroSim enforces the following technical safeguards:

  • Checksum validation on all incoming UDP datagrams to eliminate corrupted or injected packets.
  • Sliding-window token bucket rate limiting per IP to prevent denial-of-service disruptions.
  • Session tokens generated via cryptographically secure pseudo-random number generators (CSPRNG).
  • Automated database backup capabilities with SHA-256 verification.

4. Data Subject Rights & Portability

Researchers and subjects retain complete ownership over generated data files. You may export telemetry at any time via RFC 4180 CSV (/api/export/csv) or structured JSON (/api/export/json), or purge individual session records from the archive interface.

5. Contact & Data Protection Inquiries

For questions concerning data governance, HIPAA/GDPR clinical compliance, or institutional deployment, contact the system administrator or open an issue in the official project repository.
